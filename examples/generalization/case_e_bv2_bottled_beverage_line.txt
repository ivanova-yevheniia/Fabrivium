BV-2 BOTTLED BEVERAGE PACKING LINE — PRODUCT SPECIFICATION
Synthetic case document written for Fabrivium generalization validation.
Document status: SYNTHETIC CASE DOCUMENT, ISSUED FOR MANUFACTURE.

This specification describes a filled and packed bottled product. It is
deliberately a PROCESS-DOMINATED product: it has almost no assembly content
and almost no fasteners, which is the opposite shape to the discrete
assembly cases.

1. PRODUCT

The BV-2 is a 500 ml still beverage in a PET bottle with a screw closure.
The finished saleable unit is one filled, capped and labelled bottle.

The bottle is 62 mm in diameter and 215 mm tall.

2. MATERIALS

Bottles are supplied blow-moulded and arrive on tipped pallets. Closures
are supplied loose in bulk. The product liquid is delivered to the line
from an upstream batching tank that is outside the scope of this line.

3. COMPONENTS

Each saleable unit consists of:

- one PET bottle
- one screw closure
- one wrap-around body label

There are no sub-assemblies and no fasteners.

4. PROCESS

Bottles are fed from the depalletiser to the filler. Each bottle is filled
to 500 ml by volume, then capped with a screw closure and torque-checked.
A wrap-around label is applied to the bottle body.

Filling, capping and labelling are performed on a continuous-motion
monobloc.

5. QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

Every bottle passes a fill-level inspection after filling. Bottles outside
the fill tolerance are rejected from the line automatically.

The applied closure torque is verified.

6. PACKAGING

Bottles are collated and shrink-wrapped into trays of twelve. Trays are
then stacked onto pallets for despatch.

7. PRODUCTION REQUIREMENTS

The line shall produce 18,000 bottles per day across 2 shifts of 8 hours.
4 operators are available; the line is substantially automated and the
operators supervise rather than handle each unit.

The available floor area is 45 by 20 metres.

8. OPEN ITEMS

No cycle time is stated for any operation. The monobloc has not been
selected and no equipment cost is available.
