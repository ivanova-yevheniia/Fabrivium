GR-7 BENCH GRINDER GUARD ASSEMBLY — PRODUCT SPECIFICATION
Synthetic case document written for FactoryMind generalization validation.

1. PRODUCT

The GR-7 is a hinged guard assembly fitted to a bench grinder. It is a
high-volume consumable line item sold both to machine builders and as a
spare part.

Overall dimensions of the assembled guard are 180 x 140 x 95 mm.

2. MATERIALS

The shroud and the mounting bracket are pressed from steel. The viewing
window is polycarbonate.

3. COMPONENTS

Each assembly contains:

- one pressed shroud
- one mounting bracket
- one viewing window
- one warning label

4. ASSEMBLY

The viewing window is clipped into the shroud aperture. The mounting
bracket is offered up to the shroud and located on two dowels.

5. FASTENING

The bracket is fixed to the shroud with four screws driven from the inside
face.

6. QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

Every assembly receives a visual inspection covering window seating, screw
presence and the absence of sharp edges.

7. PACKAGING

Each guard is bagged and packed into a shelf carton with a fitting
instruction. The carton is closed and labelled.
