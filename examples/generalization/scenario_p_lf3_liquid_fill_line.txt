LF-3 CONSUMER LIQUID FILLING AND PACKING LINE — PRODUCT SPECIFICATION
Synthetic case document written for Fabrivium multi-domain validation.
Document status: SYNTHETIC CASE DOCUMENT, ISSUED FOR MANUFACTURE.

1. PRODUCT

The LF-3 is a consumer liquid product supplied in an individual container.
The finished saleable unit is one filled, sealed and labelled container.

The container is 68 mm in diameter and 168 mm tall.

2. MATERIALS

Containers are supplied moulded and arrive on pallets. Seals and labels are
supplied in bulk. The product liquid is delivered from an upstream batching
vessel that is outside the scope of this line.

3. COMPONENTS

Each saleable unit consists of:

- one container
- one seal
- one identification label

There are no sub-assemblies.

4. PROCESS

Containers are fed to the line from the depalletiser. Each container is
filled with the product liquid, then sealed. An identification label is
applied to the container.

5. QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

Every container receives a visual inspection after sealing. Containers
outside the fill tolerance are rejected from the line.

6. PACKAGING

Inspected containers are packed into cartons for despatch.

7. PRODUCTION REQUIREMENTS

The line shall produce 4,000 units per day across 2 shifts of 8 hours.
6 operators are available.

8. AUTOMATION

Filling, sealing and labelling are performed by automatic machinery. The
operators supervise the line and handle changeovers.

9. OPEN ITEMS

No operation cycle time has been established. No equipment has been selected
and no equipment price is available. No capital budget has been set.
