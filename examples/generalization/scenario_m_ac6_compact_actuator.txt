AC-6 COMPACT ELECTROMECHANICAL ACTUATOR — PRODUCT SPECIFICATION
Synthetic case document written for Fabrivium multi-domain validation.
Document status: SYNTHETIC CASE DOCUMENT, ISSUED FOR MANUFACTURE.

1. PRODUCT

The AC-6 is a compact electromechanical actuator for a linear positioning
duty. It is supplied assembled, lubricated and functionally checked.

The assembled unit measures 96 by 54 by 48 mm.

2. MATERIALS

The housing and the cover are machined from aluminium. The shaft is
hardened steel.

3. COMPONENTS

Each actuator contains:

- one aluminium housing
- one shaft
- one bearing
- one cover
- one identification label

4. ASSEMBLY

The bearing is pressed into the housing bore. The shaft is then inserted
through the bearing and seated against the shoulder.

The cover is secured with four screws.

5. LUBRICATION

The bearing seat is greased before the shaft is inserted.

6. QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

Every actuator is inspected for free shaft movement before release. The
inspection is a functional movement check, not a dimensional measurement.

7. IDENTIFICATION

An identification label is applied to the housing.

8. PACKAGING

Each actuator is packed individually into a carton.

9. PRODUCTION REQUIREMENTS

The line shall produce 420 units per day on 1 shift of 8 hours. 5 operators
are available.

and no equipment price is available. No capital budget has been set.
