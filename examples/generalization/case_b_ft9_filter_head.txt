FT-9 IN-LINE FILTER HEAD ASSEMBLY — PRELIMINARY PRODUCT SPECIFICATION
Synthetic case document written for FactoryMind generalization validation.
Document status: SYNTHETIC CASE DOCUMENT, PRELIMINARY RELEASE.

This specification is issued at concept stage. Several manufacturing
quantities are deliberately not yet fixed and are called out below as open.

1. PRODUCT

The FT-9 is an in-line filter head for a low-pressure coolant circuit. It
carries a replaceable cartridge and mounts to a machine frame by two
brackets.

Overall dimensions are not yet frozen. The envelope study is still open.

2. MATERIALS

Material selection for the body is still under review. Two candidates are
being evaluated by engineering and neither has been released.

3. COMPONENTS

Each assembly contains:

- one body
- one cover
- one filter cartridge seat
- one sealing ring
- one identification label

4. FASTENING

The cover is secured with screws that engage bosses in the body. The screw
count, thread and tightening torque are defined on assembly drawing
FT9-A-002, which is not released with this specification.

5. CONNECTIONS

The pressure sensor is attached with cables. The number of cables depends
on the sensor variant chosen and is not fixed at this stage.

6. QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

Each assembly is leak tested on the line and the seal seat is verified
before release. The acceptance limit for the leak test has not been agreed
with the customer.

7. PACKAGING

Finished assemblies are packed individually together with a fitting
instruction.
