DX-4 SINGLE-USE DIAGNOSTIC CASSETTE — PRODUCT SPECIFICATION
Synthetic case document written for Fabrivium generalization validation.
Document status: SYNTHETIC CASE DOCUMENT, ISSUED FOR MANUFACTURE.

This specification describes a single-use in-vitro diagnostic cassette. It
is issued for manufacturing planning. Where a quantity has not been agreed
it is stated as open rather than estimated.

1. PRODUCT

The DX-4 is a disposable lateral-flow test cassette for point-of-care use.
It is supplied sterile-packed as a single unit with a desiccant sachet and
a printed instruction leaflet.

The cassette measures 78 by 24 by 12 mm.

2. MATERIALS

The upper and lower housing shells are moulded in medical-grade
polypropylene. The nitrocellulose membrane strip and the absorbent pad are
supplied pre-cut by the reagent supplier and arrive ready to place.

3. COMPONENTS

Each cassette contains:

- one lower housing shell
- one nitrocellulose membrane strip
- one absorbent pad
- one sample pad
- one upper housing shell
- one desiccant sachet
- one identification label

The three internal pads are placed in a fixed order into the lower shell.

4. JOINING

The upper and lower shells are joined by ultrasonic welding at four weld
points. No adhesive and no fasteners are used anywhere in the product.

The weld schedule is defined on process sheet DX4-P-011.

5. FUNCTIONAL VERIFICATION

Every cassette is functionally tested before release. The test applies a
control fluid to the sample pad and reads the control line optically. The
read is pass or fail; there is no measured value to record.

Cassettes are additionally checked visually for weld integrity.

6. IDENTIFICATION

A unique identification label carrying the lot number and expiry date is
applied to the exterior of the upper shell. Labelling is required by the
product's regulatory file and cannot be omitted.

7. PACKAGING

Each cassette is sealed into an individual foil pouch together with the
desiccant sachet. Pouches are then cartoned in twenty-fives with the
instruction leaflet.

8. PRODUCTION REQUIREMENTS

The line shall produce 4,000 cassettes per day across 3 shifts of 7.5
hours. 6 operators are available. The available floor area is 22 by 14
metres.

9. OPEN ITEMS

The cycle time of the ultrasonic welding operation has not been established;
the equipment has not been selected. The capital budget has not been set.
