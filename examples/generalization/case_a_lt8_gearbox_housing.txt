LT-8 GEARBOX HOUSING ASSEMBLY — PRODUCT SPECIFICATION
Synthetic case document written for FactoryMind generalization validation.
Document status: SYNTHETIC CASE DOCUMENT. Not a customer document.

1. PRODUCT

The LT-8 is a sealed single-stage gearbox housing supplied as a finished
sub-assembly to a conveyor builder. It is an industrial-duty metal product
mounted on a driven roller frame.

Overall dimensions of the assembled unit are 310 x 240 x 180 mm.

2. MATERIALS

The housing body and the cover plate are cast in aluminium. The input shaft
and the retaining bolts are steel. No plastic parts are used anywhere on
the assembly.

3. COMPONENTS

Each assembly contains:

- one cast housing body
- one machined cover plate
- one input shaft
- two ball bearings
- one paper gasket
- one drain plug

4. PREPARATION

Both castings are washed and degreased before assembly. The machined bores
are blown dry with filtered air and the sealing faces are wiped with
solvent.

5. INTERNAL WORK

The two ball bearings are pressed into the housing bores on an arbor press.
The input shaft is fitted through the bearings and seated against the inner
race. The paper gasket is laid onto the sealing face by hand.

6. FASTENING

The cover plate is retained by twelve bolts tightened to 24 Nm in a
diagonal sequence. The drain plug is fitted with a copper washer and
tightened separately.

7. QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

Each completed assembly is pressure tested for leaks at 0.5 bar. Shaft
rotation is verified by hand before the unit leaves the cell. Assemblies
that fail are set aside for rework.

8. PACKAGING

Finished assemblies are wrapped in oiled paper and packed into a returnable
steel crate with a foam insert. Each crate holds four assemblies and is
closed with a lid strap.
