COMPACT ELECTRONICS CONTROLLER — PRODUCT SPECIFICATION
Reference document for Fabrivium demonstration purposes.
Document status: EXAMPLE / REFERENCE DATA. Not a customer document.

1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW

The product is a compact electronics controller supplied in a moulded
plastic enclosure. It is a low-voltage indoor device intended for panel or
surface mounting.

Overall dimensions of the assembled unit are 120 x 80 x 40 mm.

2. MATERIALS

The enclosure base and lid are moulded in ABS. No metal housing parts are
used.

3. COMPONENTS

Each unit contains:

- one populated printed circuit board (PCB)
- one enclosure base
- one enclosure lid
- one product label

The PCB is supplied pre-tested by the electronics supplier and is placed
into the enclosure base during final assembly.

4. INTERNAL CONNECTIONS

Two cables connect the PCB to the external terminals. Both cables use
pre-crimped connectors and are attached by hand during assembly.

5. FASTENING

The enclosure lid is secured with six screws. The screws engage moulded
bosses in the enclosure base.

Screw specification is defined in a separate fastener drawing which is not
included with this document.

6. QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

Every unit receives a visual inspection before packaging. The inspection
checks that the lid is fully closed, that both cable connectors are seated,
and that the enclosure shows no visible moulding or handling defects.

7. PACKAGING

Each finished unit is placed in a protective bag, then into a cardboard box
together with an instruction leaflet. The box is closed and labelled.
